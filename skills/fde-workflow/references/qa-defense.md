# Post-submission defense

Prepare from the frozen snapshot. For each topic, identify a concrete code location, observed evidence, and a remaining limit.

- **Design:** the most consequential choice, rejected alternative, and why the chosen boundary fits the business problem.
- **Failure:** an assumption that can be false, how the failure appears, and how a person recovers.
- **Verification:** what the tests and manual run prove, and what they do not prove.
- **Requirements:** one stakeholder fact that changed the implementation and one unresolved policy.
- **Privacy and security:** sensitive data boundary, exposure reduction, and residual risk.
- **Maintenance:** how the next operator runs, diagnoses, changes, and rolls back the workflow.
- **Tooling:** why each external Skill or MCP was necessary, what evidence it produced, and what would happen without it.

Use this answer shape:

1. Point to the specific weakness or decision in the frozen submission.
2. Explain its business and technical consequence.
3. State the next verification or improvement, without claiming it already exists.
